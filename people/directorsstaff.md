# Director and Staff

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Tor Wager | [Tor Wager, PhD](https://pbs.dartmouth.edu/people/tor-wager) **Director, Dartmouth Brain Imaging Center**  Diana L. Taylor Distinguished Professor |
| Jamie Ford | James C. Ford, PhD **Research Scientist**  James (Jamie) Ford joined the DBIC in 2024. He currently leads an imaging group in the Acute to Chronic Pain Signatures ([a2cps.org](https://a2cps.org)) project and is also involved in refining and extending DBIC's processes for storing and processing MRI scan data to ensure optimal image quality and efficiency. Jamie has worked for more than 25 years in imaging centers at Dartmouth College, the Geisel School of Medicine, and Dartmouth Health. His past work and interests in imaging include structural, functional, and diffusion MRI, EEG, and NIRS, and he is available to provide guidance and technical support to research teams using DBIC. |
|  | Michael A. Pearl, RT(R)(MR) **MRI Reserch Technologist**  Mike brings over twenty‑six years of MRI experience, most recently a decade at Dartmouth Health’s Advanced Imaging Center and Center for Surgical Innovation. He has extensive expertise in MRI safety, training, protocol development, data collection, and vendor collaboration.  In the DBIC, Mike is responsible for developing and administering the DBIC's safety and operator training programs and supporting research groups with the development, implementation, and execution of their imaging protocols. |
| Courtney Rogers | Courtney Rogers, MEd **Associate Director for Operations**  Courtney has been with the DBIC since 2008 and was promoted to her current role in March 2026.  She provides leadership for the Dartmouth Brain Imaging Center’s research, administrative, and outreach operations. She oversees day‑to‑day activities including protocol review and approval, training programs, communications, and scanner scheduling, and works closely with the Director to develop policies and support research teams and onboarding efforts. |

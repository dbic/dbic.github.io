# Research Infrastructure

The DBIC maintains a robust infrastructure in the pursuit of cutting-edge neuroscience research. We manage a state-of-the-art 3 Tesla [Siemens PRISMA scanner](fmri_scanner.md); multiple [headcoils](coils.md); [peripheral equipment](peripherals.md) for presenting tasks and recording behavioral data; an array of [computer resources](computer_resources.md) for data transfer and storage; a variety of [progamming sequences](sequences.md) to optimize data collection; and a weekly [quality assurance](qualityassurance.md) protocol to ensure scanner stability.

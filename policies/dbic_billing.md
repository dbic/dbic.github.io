# Scanning rates

## Rates

Rates for scanning effective July 1, 2021.

**Internal** funding means studies paid from Dartmouth-administered funds (for example a GL/PTAEO chart string). **External** funding means a non-Dartmouth funding source.

| Funding | When | Rate per hour |
|---|---|---|
| Internal | Peak hours (see [scan hours](/policies/scheduling.html#scanhours)) | $500 |
| Internal | Off-peak hours, including weekends (see [scan hours](/policies/scheduling.html#scanhours)) | $400 |
| External | Peak hours (see [scan hours](/policies/scheduling.html#scanhours)) | $700 |

## How billing works

Scan time is recorded in the DBIC logbook and billed on a monthly basis. The DBIC Associate Director for Operations (Courtney Rogers) emails an invoice for each DBIC protocol number (to the PI or designee) at the start of each month, covering the scans conducted in the previous month. The invoices list: scan dates, billing record, accession numbers, number of hours per scan, funding source, and the session cost for each scan. The PI (or designee) must review each invoice and verify that it is accurate. To request a correction, reply by the date given in the billing email. If Courtney Rogers does not hear back by that date, the invoice is treated as accurate and the account is charged.

Questions about billing or rates: contact [Courtney Rogers](mailto:courtney.rogers@dartmouth.edu?subject=Scanning%20rates%20and%20billing).

# DBIC parking

For approval or questions about parking, email [Courtney Rogers](mailto:courtney.rogers@dartmouth.edu?subject=DBIC%20parking).

## **Quick links**

- [Parking for **non-Dartmouth** DBIC participants](#nondart)
- [Parking for **Dartmouth-affiliated** DBIC participants](#dart)
- [Unauthorized vehicle parking](#unauthorized)

<a id="nondart"></a>

## **Non-Dartmouth research participant parking**

The DBIC has a dedicated parking spot located behind Moore Hall, reserved for **non-Dartmouth** research participants. It is on the right as you enter the lot.

If you have a **non-Dartmouth participant** who would like to use the parking spot for their visit, **please request review and approval before your participant arrives** (email Courtney Rogers, above). Please provide the following information:

- date/time of visit
- vehicle make, model and license plate

<a id="dart"></a>

## **Dartmouth-affiliated participant parking**

**Dartmouth-affiliated** research participants and vendors may not use the DBIC parking space; Safety & Security will ticket unauthorized vehicles.

Dartmouth-affiliated participants should park as follows:

1. **With a Dartmouth parking pass:** park in a [campus parking lot](../assets/transportation/parking/parking_maps/campusparkingmap1132023.pdf).
2. **No lot nearby, or no space:** use any available [visitor parking](../assets/transportation/parking/parking_maps/visitorparkingmap992022.pdf) space (pay through ParkMobile).
3. **Without a pass, parked in a permit lot (for example the Maynard Lot):** your research group emails [Transportation Services](mailto:transportation.services@dartmouth.edu?subject=) using this [email template](../assets/dbic/docs/forms/ts_email_template.docx).

<a id="unauthorized"></a>

## Unauthorized vehicle parking

If someone is parked in the DBIC spot you reserved, tell your participant to use ParkMobile parking in front of Moore Hall, or the Maynard Lot across the street (removing a vehicle can take time). Then report the vehicle:

- **Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–4 p.m.:** email [Transportation Services](mailto:Transportation.Services@dartmouth.edu) with the date, time, make, model and license plate, and copy Courtney Rogers.
- **Monday–Friday after 4 p.m. and all day Saturday and Sunday:** call Safety & Security (603-646-4000) with the make, model and license plate.

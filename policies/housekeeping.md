# Housekeeping

The MRI scanner and suite are a valuable shared resource. Beyond following all safety and operating procedures, scanner operators must complete the full [housekeeping checklist](../assets/dbic/docs/policies/housekeeping01282026.pdf). The full checklist is the authoritative list; the items below are the ones most often missed.

## Housekeeping items

### Equipment

- Make sure the projector is in standby mode when not in use.
- Leave all peripheral equipment (BIOPAC, response devices, control box, video and sound systems) neat and untangled to avoid damage.
- Return corrective lenses to their appropriate slots in the storage case, and wipe down frames with a Clorox wipe.

### The suite

- Make sure no personal belongings are left in the scanner suite.
- Restock the scrub drawers.
- Place all used linens in the designated laundry bin.
- Make sure that all trash/recycling (including earplugs/earbuds, wrappers, used tape, etc.) have been disposed of in appropriate receptacles.

### Cleaning

- Wipe down all surfaces with frequent bodily contact (e.g. inside of the head coil, response pads, emergency squeeze ball) using the DBIC-provided Clorox wipes.

### At the end of the day

- Log out of REDCap and shut down the system when your lab is the last to scan that day.

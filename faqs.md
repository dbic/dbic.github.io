# FAQs

## Scanning in the DBIC

- [How do I submit a new DBIC protocol?](policies/dbic_protocol.md)<br>Email the DBIC application, your CPHS protocol, the CPHS-stamped consent form and a description of your study design to Courtney Rogers. New protocols are reviewed before the study begins.
- [How can I sign up for DBIC safety training?](policies/safetytraining.md)<br>Complete the CITI Group 2 Social/Behavioral Basic course, email the certificate to Courtney Rogers, and choose a class from the schedule.
- [How can I sign up for DBIC operator training?](policies/operator_training.md)<br>After safety training, become an operator-in-training, complete the hands-on training hours with a certified operator, and pass a final competency check.
- [How much does it cost to scan at the DBIC?](policies/dbic_billing.md)<br>Rates depend on the funding source and time of day, and are billed monthly for each protocol.
- [How do I schedule time at the DBIC?](policies/scheduling.md)<br>With an approved protocol, email Courtney Rogers. Requests are made in monthly windows by funding tier.
- [How can I access the DBIC calendar?](policies/scheduling.md)<br>Ask Courtney Rogers for access to the Outlook calendar, or view it on the [Scheduling page](policies/scheduling.md#calendar).
- [What is the DBIC's cancellation policy?](policies/cancellations.md)<br>Booked time that is cancelled is still charged, though this charge will be reduced or waived with sufficient notice.

## Accessing computing resources

- [How do I get access to Discovery and other research computing resources?](research_infrastructure/computer_resources.md#sysaccess)<br>You can find information on the [Research Computing website](https://rc.dartmouth.edu/) and can contact Research Computing directly by emailing [research.computing@<wbr>dartmouth.edu](mailto:research.computing@dartmouth.edu) (opens a support ticket).
- [How can I use DBIC's allocation on Discovery?](research_infrastructure/discovery.md)<br>See the [Using Discovery](research_infrastructure/discovery.md) section for background and contact [Jamie Ford](mailto:james.c.ford@dartmouth.edu?subject=DBIC%20allocation%20on%20Discovery) with any questions or a request for access.
- [Where is DBIC on DartFS?](research_infrastructure/computer_resources.md#datastorage)<br>The main directory is `/dartfs/rc/lab/D/DBIC/DBIC`.
- [Where do I find my scans?](research_infrastructure/computer_resources.md#dicomstorage)<br>New scans arrive in `dbic-inbox/DICOM` under the main DBIC directory, organized by date and accession number.
- [How do I locate a scan I can see in the DBIC inbox?](research_infrastructure/computer_resources.md#datastorage)

# fMRI brown bags

The DBIC hosts a brown bag series featuring diverse topics in neuroimaging. These sessions provide opportunities to learn about emerging methods and receive feedback on ongoing projects. While most speakers are from the DBIC community, we occasionally invite speakers from the broader neuroscience field.

If you are interested in presenting or nominating an external speaker, please contact [Courtney Rogers.](mailto:courtney.rogers@dartmouth.edu?subject=fMRI%20brown%20bag)

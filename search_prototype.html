    What this shows. “Topics” are your menu entries (instant, as now). “Pages” are matches inside the text of pages, FAQ questions, datasets and documents, found with a small index that the website would build when it is published (here about 86 KB, roughly 25 KB over the network, loaded the first time someone types). In the proposed mode, Enter opens the top topic, or else the top page, and never starts an email; the email link is still there when nothing matches, but you have to choose it.
